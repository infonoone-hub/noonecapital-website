Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2025 · Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Noone Capital LLC ("Noone Capital," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address
  • Property Information: Number of units, portfolio details, investment goals
  • Communications: Messages, emails, and records of calls or texts you send us
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on our site
  • SMS Opt-In Data: Mobile phone number, consent status, timestamp, and source of opt-in

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule consultations
  • Provide property management and capital strategy services
  • Send appointment reminders, portfolio updates, and service notifications
  • Send SMS messages if you have provided explicit written consent
  • Improve our website, services, and communications
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. SMS / Text Message Communications

If you provide your mobile phone number and check the SMS consent box on our contact form, you agree to receive recurring automated text messages from Noone Capital. These messages may include:

  • Appointment reminders and confirmations
  • Portfolio performance updates
  • Property management alerts
  • Service notifications relevant to your account

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any SMS message. To request help, reply HELP or contact us at infonoone@noonecapital.net.

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or SMS consent data with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Vendors who help us operate our business (CRM, SMS platform, email service) under strict data processing agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. SMS opt-in records are retained for a minimum of 4 years as required by telecommunications regulations.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP
  • Opt out of marketing emails by clicking "unsubscribe"

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at infonoone@noonecapital.net.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to contact us immediately if you believe your information has been compromised.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on our website with a new effective date.
