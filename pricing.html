Common Questions

How fast can we go live? +

Within 48 hours of signing. We connect to your PM software, train the agents on your properties and policies, and they start handling calls, texts, and emails immediately.

What PM software do you integrate with? +

AppFolio, Buildium, Yardi, Rent Manager, and most other major platforms. If you use something else, contact us — we build custom integrations for Enterprise clients.

Do the AI agents replace my entire team? +

They handle 80–90% of all inbound communication and routine tasks. You'll still want one human manager for decisions that require legal judgment or in-person presence. Most clients go from a team of 6 to a team of 1.

Is there a contract or minimum commitment? +

Month-to-month. No long-term contracts required. We earn your business every month.

What happens if an agent can't handle something? +

Agents automatically escalate to your human manager with a full summary of the conversation. Nothing falls through the cracks — you always stay in control of the exceptions.
