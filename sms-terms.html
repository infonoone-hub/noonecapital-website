1. Program Description

Noone Capital LLC ("Noone Capital," "we," "us") operates an SMS messaging program to communicate with clients and prospective clients who have provided explicit written consent. This program delivers transactional and informational messages related to property management services and client account activity.

2. How to Opt In

You may opt in to receive SMS messages from Noone Capital by:

  • Checking the SMS consent checkbox on our website contact form at noonecapital.com and providing your mobile phone number
  • Texting START to our designated business number
  • Providing written consent during an onboarding call or meeting

By opting in, you confirm that you are the account holder or have the account holder's permission to enroll the number, and that you are 18 years of age or older.

3. Types of Messages You Will Receive

After opting in, you may receive the following types of text messages from Noone Capital:

  • Appointment reminders and confirmations for strategy sessions or property visits
  • Monthly portfolio performance summaries
  • Maintenance and work order status updates
  • Rent collection and payment reminders
  • Lease renewal notices and deadlines
  • Vacancy and marketing updates for your properties
  • Important account or service notifications

4. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your account activity and the services you receive. Clients typically receive between 2 and 6 messages per month. You will never receive unsolicited promotional spam.

5. Message and Data Rates

Standard message and data rates from your mobile carrier may apply to messages sent and received. Noone Capital does not charge for SMS messages. Contact your mobile carrier for details about your messaging plan.

6. How to Opt Out

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time using any of the following methods:

  • Reply STOP, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, END, or QUIT to any SMS message from us
  • Email infonoone@noonecapital.net with "SMS Opt-Out" in the subject line
  • Call us at 612-220-0860 and request removal

After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message. You will not receive further SMS messages unless you opt in again. Opting out of SMS does not affect your service agreement with Noone Capital.

7. How to Get Help

For assistance with our SMS program:

  • Reply HELP to any SMS message
  • Email infonoone@noonecapital.net
  • Call 612-220-0860 during business hours (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm ET)

8. Supported Carriers

Our SMS program supports all major US carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, Boost Mobile, US Cellular, and others. Carrier support may vary. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

9. Privacy of Your Information

Your mobile phone number and consent information will never be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes. It is used solely to deliver the messages described in this program and to maintain opt-in records as required by law. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

10. Consent Is Not Required

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any product or service from Noone Capital. You may use all of our services without agreeing to receive text messages.

11. Changes to This Program

We reserve the right to modify or terminate this SMS program at any time. We will notify enrolled participants of material changes via SMS or email before changes take effect.
